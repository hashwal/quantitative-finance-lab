# Research standards

Each project should document its question, theory, data provenance, assumptions, methods, tests, results and limitations. Distinguish synthetic examples from empirical findings. Use only public or appropriately licensed data; never publish employer/client information, secrets or restricted datasets. For predictive work use chronological out-of-sample validation, account for trading costs, and assess look-ahead and survivorship bias. Record package versions and random seeds where relevant. Educational research is not investment advice.
